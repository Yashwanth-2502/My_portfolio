04 / My Process

How I work
through
problems.

My approach blends analytical rigor with creative problem-solving. Every project begins with understanding the human need behind the data.

Step 01

Understand the Problem

Start by asking the right questions — understanding business context, stakeholder needs, and what success actually looks like.

Step 02

Collect & Clean Data

Gathering from multiple sources, applying rigorous preprocessing — because garbage in means garbage out. Always.

Step 03

Explore & Hypothesize

Deep EDA to uncover patterns, anomalies, and relationships that aren't visible on the surface.

Step 04

Model & Iterate

Building, training, evaluating — iterating until the model performs reliably across diverse test conditions.

Step 05

Visualize & Deliver

Translating technical findings into clear, actionable insights that non-technical stakeholders can actually use.
